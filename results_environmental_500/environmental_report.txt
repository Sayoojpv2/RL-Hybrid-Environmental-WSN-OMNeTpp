(1 round = 1 h of environmental time; 100 s of OMNeT++ scheduling time), 2 J radio energy per node, first-order radio,
tabular Q-learning RL-Hybrid with variant C (energyGuard 0.8), no retransmission. Only the scenario workload changes.
Every report is one 802.15.4 frame of its own profile (only its present fields); energy, feasibility and airtime use the
actual frame bits. Sensing energy is a separate ledger, never charged to the 2 J battery: lifetimes are communication-network
lifetimes. A run seed sets topology + channel + RL stream, identical in every scenario.
